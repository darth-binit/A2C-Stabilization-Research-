# Stabilizing Actor-Critic Learning for Stochastic Disaster Relief Logistics under Partial Observability

## Overview

This repository accompanies the research manuscript:

**"Stabilizing Actor-Critic Learning for Stochastic Disaster Relief Logistics under Partial Observability"**

The project investigates the stability of actor-critic reinforcement learning in stochastic disaster relief logistics environments where the decision-maker operates under partial observability and faces uncertainty in demand, supply, transportation, and operational conditions.

## Research Motivation

Disaster relief logistics involves sequential decision-making under highly uncertain and dynamic conditions. Conventional reinforcement learning methods can experience unstable learning and policy updates when the environment is stochastic and only partially observable.

This research therefore focuses on improving the stability and robustness of actor-critic learning in such environments.

## Research Problem

The study investigates how actor-critic reinforcement learning can be made more stable when:

- the environment is stochastic;
- the decision-maker has only partial information about the underlying state;
- operational conditions change over time; and
- learning is affected by noisy and uncertain observations.

## Methodological Approach

The research develops and evaluates a stability-oriented actor-critic reinforcement learning framework for stochastic disaster relief logistics.

The study considers:

1. **Stochastic disaster relief logistics environments**
2. **Partial observability**
3. **Actor-critic reinforcement learning**
4. **Learning stability and policy behavior**
5. **Experimental evaluation under uncertain conditions**

The framework is evaluated against relevant reinforcement learning baselines to examine whether the proposed stabilization approach improves learning behavior and decision-making performance.

## Application Domain

The primary application domain is **disaster relief logistics**, where reinforcement learning can support sequential decisions involving the allocation and movement of limited resources under uncertain and changing conditions.
